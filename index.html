I'm Andy Aketch, a Geomatic Engineering graduate based in Nairobi, Kenya, working at the intersection of spatial data science, remote sensing, and software development.

I build end-to-end geospatial pipelines — from processing satellite imagery in Google Earth Engine and running machine learning models in Python, to designing spatial databases in PostGIS and delivering results through interactive web maps.

My work spans land cover change analysis, ecological quality assessment, network accessibility modelling, and field survey, with a focus on translating complex spatial data into outputs that are clear and actionable. I'm currently expanding into full-stack development to bridge the gap between geospatial analysis and the web applications that make it accessible.
